# Power of the pre-registered analysis (simulation, seed 'bench-power-v1', 2000 sims per point)
# Monte Carlo error of a power near 0.8: about +/- 0.009

## A. Smallest standardized paired effect detected with 80% power
Primary test: two-sided exact Wilcoxon signed-rank on task-level differences.
dz = mean of task differences / SD of task differences (normal differences).
- alpha 0.05:
  n=20 tasks: dz = 0.68 (Wilcoxon power 0.80, paired t at same dz 0.82)
  n=25 tasks: dz = 0.59 (Wilcoxon power 0.80, paired t at same dz 0.82)
  n=30 tasks: dz = 0.54 (Wilcoxon power 0.80, paired t at same dz 0.81)
- alpha 0.0167 (Holm, 3 tests, worst case):
  n=20 tasks: dz = 0.82 (Wilcoxon power 0.80, paired t at same dz 0.83)
  n=25 tasks: dz = 0.70 (Wilcoxon power 0.80, paired t at same dz 0.83)
  n=30 tasks: dz = 0.64 (Wilcoxon power 0.80, paired t at same dz 0.81)
